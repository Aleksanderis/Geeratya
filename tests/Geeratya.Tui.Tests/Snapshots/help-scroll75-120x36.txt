  Ctrl-p     Previous source in the picker                                                                              
  Down       Next source in the picker                                                                                  
  Ctrl-n     Next source in the picker                                                                                  
  Tab        Next field of the form                                                                                     
  Shift-Tab  Previous field of the form                                                                                 
  Ctrl-s     Save the form                                                                                              
  Ctrl-o     Open the issue page in the browser from the form                                                           
                                                                                                                        
Palette commands                                                                                                        
  :jql <query>                                                                                                          
      Open a JQL query in a new tab                                                                                     
  :filter [name]                                                                                                        
      Open a saved filter in a new tab                                                                                  
  :board [name]                                                                                                         
      Open a board as columns in a new tab                                                                              
  :backlog [name]                                                                                                       
      Open a board's backlog in a new tab                                                                               
  :tab new|close|next|prev|<n>                                                                                          
      Open the source picker, close or switch tabs                                                                      
  :labels                                                                                                               
      Edit the labels of the selected or shown issue (field form)                                                       
  :profile add|list|switch <name>|remove <name>|check                                                                   
      Manage Jira profiles                                                                                              
  :set hints|whichkey on|off                                                                                            
      Show or hide the status bar hints or the which-key popup (saved)                                                  
  :help                                                                                                                 
      Show or hide help                                                                                                 
  :quit                                                                                                                 
      Quit
